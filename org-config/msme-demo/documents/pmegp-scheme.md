# PMEGP Scheme Guide

## Overview
The Prime Minister's Employment Generation Programme (PMEGP) is a credit-linked subsidy scheme for new micro-enterprises.

## Subsidy Structure
| Category | Urban | Rural |
|----------|-------|-------|
| General | 15% | 25% |
| SC/ST/OBC/Women/PH | 25% | 35% |

Maximum project cost: Rs. 50 lakh (manufacturing) or Rs. 20 lakh (service).

## Eligibility
- Any individual above 18 years
- Self Help Groups, Charitable Trusts
- Only one person per family eligible

## How to Apply
1. Visit kviconline.gov.in/pmegpeportal
2. Register and fill online application
3. Bank sanctions loan after scrutiny
4. Subsidy released through KVIC/KVIB/DIC
