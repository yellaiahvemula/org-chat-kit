# UDYAM Registration Guide

## Overview
UDYAM Registration is the official MSME registration process launched on July 1, 2020. Registration is free, paperless, and based on self-declaration.

## Benefits
1. Priority sector lending — Banks allocate 40% of advances to priority sectors including MSMEs
2. Access to PMEGP, CGTMSE, MUDRA, and Stand-Up India schemes
3. Protection against delayed payments under MSME Development Act
4. Concession on electricity bills in many states
5. Tender preferences in government procurement

## Classification (Revised 2020)
- **Micro:** Investment up to Rs. 1 crore, turnover up to Rs. 5 crore
- **Small:** Investment up to Rs. 10 crore, turnover up to Rs. 50 crore
- **Medium:** Investment up to Rs. 50 crore, turnover up to Rs. 250 crore

## Documents Required
1. Aadhaar number of proprietor/partner/director
2. PAN card of the enterprise
3. Bank account details (account number and IFSC)
4. NIC code for business activity
5. Investment and turnover details (self-declared)

## Process
1. Visit udyamregistration.gov.in
2. Enter Aadhaar and validate with OTP
3. Fill enterprise details
4. Submit — certificate generated instantly

Processing time: Instant.
