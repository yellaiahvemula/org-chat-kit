# MUDRA Loan Scheme

## Overview
MUDRA provides loans up to Rs. 10 lakh to non-corporate, non-farm micro enterprises.

## Categories
- **Shishu:** Up to Rs. 50,000
- **Kishore:** Rs. 50,001 to Rs. 5,00,000
- **Tarun:** Rs. 5,00,001 to Rs. 10,00,000

## Interest Rates
Typically 8-12% per annum. No collateral required up to Rs. 10 lakh.

## How to Apply
Approach any bank branch, NBFC, or MFI with business plan and KYC documents.
